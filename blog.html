Early Signs of Cerebral Palsy Every Parent Should Know

Every parent treasures a baby's first smile, first roll and first steps. Sometimes, though, a child's movements or milestones seem different from what is expected, and worry follows. Knowing the early signs of cerebral palsy (CP) helps families ask the right questions at the right time.

Therapy Guide

How Physiotherapy Helps Children with CP

After a cerebral palsy (CP) diagnosis, many parents ask the same question: how can we help our child move more comfortably and confidently? Physiotherapy for children with cerebral palsy is often a key part of the answer. It cannot cure CP, but it can support strength, movement, comfort and participation in everyday life.

Parent Care

Daily Care Tips for Parents of Special Children

Caring for a child with cerebral palsy or another developmental disability brings deep love, and also long days, constant decisions and quiet worries. There is no perfect routine, but small, steady habits can make life smoother for your child and for you. These daily care tips for parents of special children are general ideas, so adapt them with guidance from your child's doctor and therapists.

Understanding Autism and Developmental Delays

Every child grows at their own pace, but sometimes a parent senses that something feels different. Perhaps a toddler is not talking yet, or doesn't respond when called. Understanding autism and developmental delays helps families move from worry to informed action, without blame or fear.

Home Therapy

Best Home Exercises for Children with CP

When a child has cerebral palsy (CP), progress often depends on what happens between therapy sessions. Regular practice at home can help children build strength, balance and confidence. Parents often ask which home exercises for children with cerebral palsy are best. The honest answer is that the best exercises are the ones a qualified therapist chooses for your child.
