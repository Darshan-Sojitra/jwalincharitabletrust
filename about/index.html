Founder's message

Why we started

Harsh Bhalala
Founder, Jwalin Charitable Trust

“From our journey to every family's hope.”

Every meaningful journey begins with a purpose. For me, that purpose is my son, Jwalin.

When Jwalin was diagnosed with cerebral palsy, our family stepped into a world filled with uncertainty. We experienced firsthand the emotional, physical and financial challenges that countless families face every day — from finding the right diagnosis and treatment options to understanding therapy, government benefits and educational support, and simply knowing where to turn for reliable guidance.

Throughout this journey, we also realised that families affected by cerebral palsy need much more than medical care. They need awareness, acceptance, respect, emotional support and a compassionate community that truly understands their struggles. Dedicated resources and organised guidance for cerebral palsy families remain limited, while social awareness and sensitivity toward children with special needs still have a long way to grow.

Inspired by my beloved son, Jwalin, and guided by the divine blessings of Lord Krishna, I established Jwalin Charitable Trust with a simple yet powerful vision — to ensure that no family walks this journey alone.

Our mission is to become a trusted source of guidance, hope and support for children with cerebral palsy and their families. We strive to provide reliable information about diagnosis, therapies, rehabilitation, government schemes, education and available resources, while extending financial assistance, medical support and emotional care wherever possible.

Beyond providing services, our dream is to build a compassionate community — bringing together like-minded individuals, healthcare professionals, therapists, educators, volunteers, donors and organisations who share the same spirit of service. Together, we can create a future where every child is valued, every parent feels supported and every family has access to the opportunities they deserve.

Jwalin Charitable Trust is not just an organisation — it is our family's promise to transform our own experiences into hope for thousands of others. If our journey can make even one family's path a little easier, our purpose will be fulfilled.

I warmly invite you to join us in this mission. Together, with compassion, unity and God's grace, we can make a meaningful difference in the lives of children with cerebral palsy and build a more inclusive, caring and hopeful society.

With gratitude and faith,
Harsh Bhalala
Founder, Jwalin Charitable Trust

Who we are

Jwalin Charitable Trust is a humanitarian initiative dedicated to supporting children affected by Cerebral Palsy, developmental disabilities and mental disorders in India. Our mission is to create awareness, improve access to therapy and support systems, and empower families with knowledge, compassion and hope.

We understand that families caring for special children face emotional, financial and social challenges. Through our programs and community efforts, we aim to reduce these burdens and help every child receive the care and opportunities they deserve.

Our mission

To transform the lives of children with cerebral palsy and their families by providing comprehensive guidance, compassionate support and meaningful opportunities.

Our vision

To build a world where every child with cerebral palsy is empowered to live with dignity, confidence and opportunity, and where every family receives the knowledge, support, acceptance and care they need to thrive.
